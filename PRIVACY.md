# Flexify Privacy Policy

Flexify is made by Hassan Irfan.

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Flexify is a browser extension that adds tools to the FAST-NUCES FlexStudent portal (`flexstudent.nu.edu.pk`). It is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by FAST-NUCES.

## What Flexify reads

When **you** open a FlexStudent page, Flexify reads the academic information shown on that page:

- University information: degree, campus, batch, section, enrolment status and the academic calendar
- Your name, roll number and profile photo (shown on Flexify's Home tab, and used to sign the query emails you choose to write)
- Transcript: courses, grades, credit hours and semester totals
- Marks, attendance, your tentative study plan and your course-feedback list

Flexify does **not** read the Personal, Contact or Family sections of your profile, your fee payment details, or your password. It never logs in for you and never opens Flex pages on its own.

## Where your data goes

Nowhere. Everything Flexify reads is stored **only on your device**, in your browser's extension storage. Flexify:

- has no servers and makes no network requests of its own. When you press **Download admit card** or **Fill & submit all** feedback, Flexify acts inside your open Flex tab exactly as your own clicks would, using your own session;
- includes no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party code;
- never sells, shares or transfers your data to anyone.

## Course feedback

Feedback is only submitted after you press **Fill & submit all** and then **Confirm**. Flexify fills each pending course's form with the rating you chose and submits it in your Flex tab, one form at a time. It stops when you press Stop, after 15 minutes, or if Flex rejects a form.

## Query emails

When you use **Open in Gmail**, Flexify opens a Gmail compose window with the text you reviewed. Nothing is sent until you press Send in Gmail yourself. **Copy text** only copies to your clipboard. The teacher email addresses you enter are saved on your device so you don't have to type them again.

## Settings

Your settings (on/off, dark mode, course limits, fee per credit, saved teacher emails) are stored on your device only.

## Deleting your data

Open Flexify → **Settings** → **Clear all saved data**, or remove the extension. Removing it deletes everything it stored.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
